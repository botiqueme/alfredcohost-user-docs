# Objections — Answers

Use these answers when the visitor raises a concern or objection.

---

## I don't have time to set this up

Alfred is live in about 10 minutes with a base configuration. You connect your channels, walk through the guided setup with clear examples and a ready-made structure — no blank pages. You can add more property detail later at your own pace; the service runs from minute one. Start now, complete when you want.

---

## I'm not technical

You don't need to be. Alfred guides you step by step with clear examples and practical suggestions. No coding, no technical skills, no onboarding team. Most hosts complete base setup in a single sitting. If you can fill in your property details, you can set up Alfred.

---

## What if Alfred gets something wrong?

Alfred handles 100% of digital guest interactions and escalates to you only when physical presence is needed — a lockout, a maintenance issue, something that requires a human on-site. You stay in control: the dashboard gives you full visibility into every conversation and escalation. Alfred is built to handle with contextual accuracy and escalate when needed, not to hide problems.

---

## I don't want to lose control

You don't. You stay in command; Alfred does the work. You set the rules — property information, access controls, when guests see what. Alfred alerts your phone only when you need to step in. The dashboard shows every conversation and escalation. Your role shifts from reactive message-answering to strategic oversight.

---

## Is my data safe?

Your property information is protected. You decide who can access what, and guests see only what they need, when they need it. Access codes aren't shared before check-in; sensitive details stay private. You set the rules; Alfred enforces them.

---

## What percentage of messages does it actually handle?

Alfred handles 100% of digital guest interactions across your connected channels. Not "most" or "up to" a percentage — every message. The only time your phone rings is when something requires your physical presence. That's the product architecture, not a target.

---

## How is this different from a chatbot?

Chatbots follow scripts and need you to write every answer. Alfred is an autonomous AI Receptionist: it reads guest messages, understands context using your property information, and responds on its own — in the guest's language, in seconds, 24/7. No scripts, no templates, no approval step. You're only involved when physical intervention is needed.

---

## I have a co-host / team

Alfred handles the communication so your co-host or team can focus on what humans do best — physical tasks, guest experience on-site, or property maintenance. You get one system for all guest messages, consistent answers across the journey, and full visibility in the dashboard. Alfred replaces the receptionist function; it gives your team time back.

---

## I only have one or two properties

Alfred is built for solo hosts and property managers alike. Same 10-minute setup, same 100% handling, same full journey coverage. You get your evenings and weekends back without hiring anyone. Many hosts with one or two properties see the biggest relief because they're the ones currently answering every message themselves.
