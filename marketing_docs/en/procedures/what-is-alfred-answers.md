## What is Alfred? (60 seconds)

If you run short-term rentals, you know the problem: guests message constantly, across every platform, at every hour. You're either glued to your phone or risking bad reviews from slow responses.

Alfred is the AI Receptionist built for this. It connects to your Airbnb, Booking.com, WhatsApp, and email, then handles 100% of guest interactions autonomously — from pre-booking inquiries through post-stay follow-up. Not canned responses. Not drafts for you to approve. Alfred reads, understands, and responds with the right information, in the guest's language, in seconds.

When something needs a human — a broken lock, a medical situation — Alfred calls your phone. Everything else, it handles. Setup takes about 10 minutes. No technical skills required. You stay in control through the dashboard, but you no longer do the work.
