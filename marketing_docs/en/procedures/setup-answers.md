# Setup — Answers

Use these answers when the visitor asks about setup time, difficulty, or what they need to do.

---

## How long does setup take?

Base setup takes about 10 minutes. You connect your channels and go through Alfred's guided setup with clear examples and a ready-made structure — no blank pages. You can add more property detail later at your own pace; Alfred starts handling guests from day one. Start now, complete when you want.

---

## Is it hard to set up?

No. Alfred guides you step by step with clear examples and practical suggestions. You don't need technical skills or a developer. Most hosts complete base setup in one sitting. You're not filling in a blank form — you're confirming and enriching what Alfred has already prepared.

---

## What do I need to do to get started?

Connect your listing — Airbnb, Booking.com, WhatsApp, email — add your property information with Alfred's guidance, and you're live. Alfred then handles guest messages 24/7. You can refine details anytime. No coding, no technical team, no weeks of configuration.

---

## Do I need to be technical?

No. Alfred's setup is designed so that every step makes sense and every option has a sensible default. You're never left staring at a settings page wondering what to do next. Guided setup, clear examples, no blank pages. You go from signup to live in about 10 minutes.

---

## Can I add more detail later?

Yes. You start with a base configuration and Alfred starts working immediately. Every detail you add — property quirks, local recommendations, house rules — makes Alfred smarter. The service runs from minute one; you don't have to finish everything to benefit.

---

## Live in 10 minutes. Complete at your pace.

Start with a base setup and Alfred starts working immediately. Add detail when you have time — the service never stops.
