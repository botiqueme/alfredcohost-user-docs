# How Alfred is different — Answers

Use these answers when the visitor asks how Alfred compares to other solutions or what makes Alfred different. Do not name other companies. Contrast by category.

---

## How is Alfred different?

Alfred is an autonomous AI Receptionist — not a chatbot, not a messaging template, not an assistant that needs your approval. Alfred reads every guest message across every connected channel, understands what the guest needs using your property's actual information, and responds in seconds, in the guest's language. It covers the entire stay journey: pre-booking questions, booking confirmations, pre-arrival details, during-stay support, checkout instructions, and post-stay follow-up. Your phone rings only when something requires physical human action. Everything else, Alfred manages.

---

## Alfred vs template-based tools

Unlike template-based tools that send scheduled messages but can't answer unscripted questions, Alfred handles 100% of guest interactions autonomously. Alfred uses your property information to give contextual answers in real time — not pre-written replies. Every message, every channel, every language.

---

## Alfred vs draft-and-review tools

Unlike tools where the AI suggests a response and you have to review and click send, Alfred reads, decides, and responds on its own. No approval step. No queue of messages waiting for you in the morning. Alfred works 24/7; you're only involved when physical intervention is needed.

---

## Why 100% matters

Many tools automate only part of guest communication and still need constant oversight. Alfred is built for 100% of digital guest interactions across your connected channels. The strongest alternatives in the market report 70–90% automation; Alfred's architecture was built for full autonomy. That's not a goal — it's the product.

---

## Setup speed

Alfred is live with a base configuration in about 10 minutes. No weeks of manual property setup, no technical onboarding team. Some other solutions require weeks or "days" to get started. Alfred removes the biggest adoption barrier: you can start now and add detail at your own pace.

---

## You stay in command

Alfred doesn't "replace you" — it replaces the receptionist function. You set the rules; Alfred executes. You have full visibility in the dashboard and your phone rings when you need to step in. Your role shifts from answering every message to strategic oversight.
