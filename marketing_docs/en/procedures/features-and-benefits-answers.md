# Features and benefits — Answers

Use these answers when the visitor asks what Alfred does, what they get, or about specific capabilities.

---

## What does Alfred do?

Alfred handles every guest message across your connected channels — Airbnb, Booking.com, VRBO, WhatsApp, email. Pre-booking questions, check-in instructions, Wi-Fi and house rules, local recommendations, checkout reminders, post-stay follow-up. Alfred responds in seconds, in the guest's language, 24/7. When something needs you in person, Alfred alerts your phone. Everything else, Alfred manages.

---

## What channels does Alfred work with?

Alfred connects to Airbnb, Booking.com, VRBO, WhatsApp, and email. One system covers all your platforms. Alfred works with any property management system — you keep your existing tools and add Alfred on top.

---

## Does Alfred work 24/7?

Yes. Alfred responds in seconds at any hour, in any language the guest writes. No draft-and-review, no queue for the morning. The host sleeps; Alfred works.

---

## What languages does Alfred support?

Alfred responds in the guest's language automatically. Native multilingual — not mechanical translation. The guest writes in their language; Alfred answers in their language.

---

## How fast does Alfred respond?

Alfred responds in seconds. No human queue, no review delay. Guests get answers right away.

---

## What is the guest journey Alfred covers?

Alfred covers six stages: pre-booking, booking confirmation, pre-arrival, during stay, checkout, and post-stay. From first inquiry to final review — every touchpoint, every connected channel.

---

## What happens when Alfred can't answer?

Alfred escalates to you. Your phone rings when physical presence is needed — a lockout, a maintenance issue, something that requires a human on-site. The guest gets a reassuring message; you get the alert. The dashboard shows every conversation and escalation so you're always in the loop.

---

## Is my property information safe?

Yes. Your property information is protected. You set access rules; guests see only what they need, when they need it. Access codes aren't shared before check-in. Sensitive details stay private. You set the rules; Alfred enforces them.

---

## Do I need to update Alfred?

No. Alfred's technology stays current without you doing anything. The team behind Alfred keeps the system updated so you always run the latest version.
