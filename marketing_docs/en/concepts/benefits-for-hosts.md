# What you get with Alfred

## Your time back

Alfred gives you your time back by handling 100% of guest communications autonomously. You run your business, not your inbox. No more being glued to your phone. No more midnight messages. No more repeating check-in instructions for the hundredth time. Alfred handles it; you get your evenings and weekends back.

---

## Every message handled

Every guest message is handled without you taking action. Check-in times, Wi-Fi passwords, late-night questions, local recommendations — Alfred responds in seconds, in the guest's language, 24/7. No guest waits hours for an answer. That means better review scores and no "slow response" penalties. Your guests get instant, thoughtful responses; you get relief from constant interruption.

---

## One system for all your channels

Alfred connects to Airbnb, Booking.com, VRBO, WhatsApp, and email. One system covers all platforms. You stop switching between apps and repeating yourself. Your guest experience stays consistent no matter where they booked. Alfred works with your existing property management system — you keep your tools and add Alfred on top.

---

## Live in 10 minutes

Base setup takes about 10 minutes. You connect your channels and walk through Alfred's guided setup with clear examples and a ready-made structure. No blank pages. No technical skills required. You can add more property detail later at your own pace; Alfred starts handling guests from day one. Start now, complete when you want.

---

## You intervene only when it matters

Alfred alerts your phone only when physical presence is needed — a lockout, a maintenance issue, something no AI should handle alone. Everything else, Alfred manages. You stay in control: the dashboard shows every conversation and escalation. You're not in the dark; you're in command.

---

## Nothing falls through the cracks

Alfred covers the full guest journey: pre-booking, booking confirmation, pre-arrival, during stay, checkout, post-stay. Every touchpoint, every channel. No message goes unanswered. Your guests get a consistent experience from first contact to final review. You get the peace of mind that nothing is missed.

---

## Your property information stays yours

You decide who sees what. Access controls ensure guests get only the information relevant to their stay, and only at the right time. Access codes aren't shared before check-in. Sensitive details stay private. You set the rules; Alfred enforces them.

---

## Always current, without the effort

Alfred's technology improves over time without you doing anything. No upgrades to install, no obsolescence anxiety. You focus on your property; the team behind Alfred keeps the system running at its best.

---

## For solo hosts

Alfred handles it so you don't have to. Whether you have one listing or three, you get the same 100% handling and 10-minute setup. No hiring, no co-host required for messaging. You get your life back.

---

## For property managers

Scale your guest experience without scaling staff. Alfred covers the full journey across all properties and channels. One dashboard, consistent responses, smart escalation. Your team focuses on operations, maintenance, and guest experience where humans add the most value; Alfred handles the communication workload.
