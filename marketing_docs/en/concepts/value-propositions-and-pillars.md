# Alfred's value and how it works

## Your AI Receptionist. Your guests handled. Your life back.

Alfred responds to your guests so you can focus on what matters. Alfred handles 100% of interactions across your connected channels and alerts you when you need to step in. You can start with a base setup in about 10 minutes and add more detail at your own pace — the service runs from day one.

---

## Total autonomy

Alfred handles 100% of guest communications. No review, no approval, no intervention. Every message, every channel, every language — Alfred reads, decides, and responds. Your phone rings only when physical presence is needed: a lockout, a maintenance emergency, a situation that needs a human on-site. You get your time back; Alfred works 24/7.

---

## Effortless activation

Base configuration is live in about 10 minutes. Alfred guides you step by step with clear examples and a ready-made structure — no blank pages, no PDFs to fill. You don't need technical skills or a developer. Start basic, add depth over time; the service never stops. Connect your channels and Alfred starts handling guests.

---

## Full guest journey

From first inquiry to final review — every stage, every channel, every language. Pre-booking questions, booking confirmation, pre-arrival details, during-stay support, checkout, post-stay follow-up. Airbnb, Booking.com, VRBO, WhatsApp, email. Alfred responds in the guest's language automatically and uses your actual property information — directions, rules, recommendations — so answers are contextual, not generic.

---

## You stay in command

You set the rules; Alfred does the work. Smart escalation means your phone rings only when you need to step in. Your property information is protected; guests see only what they need, when they need it. The dashboard gives you full visibility into every conversation and escalation. Alfred's technology stays current without you doing anything. Your role is strategic oversight, not answering the same questions at midnight.
