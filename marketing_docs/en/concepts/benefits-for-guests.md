# What your guests get

## Instant, accurate answers

Your guests get answers in seconds, not hours. Check-in details, Wi-Fi, house rules, local tips — Alfred responds with the right information, in their language, at any time of day. No more waiting for you to wake up or finish work. No message goes unanswered.

---

## The same experience, whatever channel they use

Whether they booked on Airbnb, Booking.com, WhatsApp, or direct, your guests get the same quality of communication. One consistent voice, one place for every question. They don't feel the difference between channels; they just get the answer they need.

---

## Answers in their language

Alfred responds in the guest's language automatically. No mechanical translation — Alfred understands and answers in the language they write. International guests get the same level of care without you having to translate or outsource.

---

## Support across the whole stay

From the first question before booking through post-stay, your guests are covered. Pre-arrival logistics, during-stay questions, checkout instructions, follow-up — every stage is handled. They're never left without a clear answer.

---

## Real information about your property

Alfred uses your actual property information: directions, rules, recommendations, quirks. Guests get contextual answers that match your listing, not generic replies. That builds confidence and reduces confusion.

---

## Calm when something needs you

When a question needs your input, the guest gets a reassuring response — for example, "Let me confirm that detail and get right back to you" — not an alarming dead end. Alfred keeps the tone confident and helpful so the guest knows they're in good hands.

---

## Your brand, your voice

Guests interact with what feels like you. Alfred operates as invisible infrastructure: no third-party branding, no "powered by" labels. Your property, your communication, your guest experience.
