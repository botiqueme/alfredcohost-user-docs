# What is Alfred?

Alfred is the Autonomous AI Receptionist for short-term rentals. Alfred handles 100% of guest interactions across the entire stay journey — from the first inquiry to post-stay — and escalates to you only when something needs your physical presence. You run your guest communication; you run your hospitality business.

Alfred is not a chatbot. Chatbots follow scripts and need you to write every answer. Alfred reads guest messages, understands what they need using your property information, and responds on its own — in the guest's language, in seconds, 24/7. No scripts, no approval step. Your phone rings only when a lockout, a maintenance issue, or something that requires a human on-site.

Alfred is built for hospitality: short-term rentals, hotels, B&Bs. It connects to your existing channels — Airbnb, Booking.com, VRBO, WhatsApp, email — and works with any property management system. You stay in control through the dashboard; Alfred does the work.

## What Alfred does

Alfred handles every guest message across your connected channels. Pre-booking questions, check-in instructions, Wi-Fi and house rules, local recommendations, checkout reminders, post-stay follow-up — all of it. Alfred responds in seconds, in the language the guest writes. When something needs you in person, Alfred alerts your phone. Everything else, Alfred manages.

## Why Alfred is different

Many tools only automate part of guest communication and still need you to review or reply. Some send scheduled messages but can't answer unscripted questions. Alfred is built for full autonomy: it reads, decides, and responds independently. The strongest alternatives in the market report 70–90% automation; Alfred handles 100% of digital guest interactions across your connected channels. Your role shifts from reactive message-answering to strategic oversight.
